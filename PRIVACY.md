# Privacy Policy for Bubuya School App

Effective Date: April 2026

The Bubuya School App is designed to support students, teachers, and the community with educational resources and school updates. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you provide.

## Information We Collect
- The app does not collect personal information such as names, emails, or phone numbers.
- Any student records or data entered remain stored locally on the user’s device.
- We do not use third‑party analytics or advertising services.

## How We Use Information
- Data entered by users is used only for educational purposes within the app.
- No information is shared externally or transmitted to servers.

## Children’s Privacy
- The app is intended for educational use by students and teachers.
- No personal data is collected from children.

## Contact Us
If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the school administration at Bubuya Community Secondary School, Kambia District, Sierra Leone.
