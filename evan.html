Privacy Policy for EvanHub

Last updated: September 26, 2026

EvanHub ("we", "our", "us") is a multi-tenant school management application that allows schools to register, manage students, manage teachers, store school documents, and access paid subscription features. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using EvanHub, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

EvanHub is used by school administrators, teachers, and (where applicable) parents/guardians. Depending on how the app is used, we may collect:

  • School account information: school name, address, contact phone number, contact email, and subscription/plan details.
  • Administrator/staff account information: name, email address, role, and login credentials.
  • Student records: name, class/grade, enrollment details, and other academic information entered by the school.
  • Teacher records: name, contact details, and role/assignment information entered by the school.
  • School documents: files and documents uploaded by school administrators (e.g. announcements, records, and related materials).
  • Payment and subscription information: EvanHub offers paid subscription plans. Payments are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card details — this information is handled directly and securely by Google. We only receive confirmation of subscription status (e.g. active, expired, plan type).
  • Device and usage information: basic technical data such as device type, operating system, app version, and crash/analytics data used to improve app performance and stability.
